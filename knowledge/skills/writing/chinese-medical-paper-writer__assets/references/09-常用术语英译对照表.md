## 十一、英文摘要常用句式模板

### 目的句

- "To investigate [sth] / [sth] and to explore [sth]."
- "To analyze [sth] and to evaluate its [sth]."

### 方法句

- "A total of XX patients with [diagnosis] admitted to [XX hospital] between [date] and [date] were retrospectively analyzed."
- "Clinical data of [sth] were collected and analyzed."
- "[Statistical method] was used for [sth]. P<0.05 was considered statistically significant."

### 结果句

- "The incidence of [sth] was XX% (XX/XX)."
- "[A] was significantly higher/lower in [B group] than in [C group] ([value] vs [value], P<0.05)."
- "Multivariate Logistic regression analysis showed that [A] was an independent risk factor for [B] (OR=XX, 95%CI: XX-XX, P=0.XX)."

### 结论句

- "[Sth] may improve the outcome of [sth]."
- "[Sth] is associated with [sth] in [population]."
- "Our findings suggest that [sth] may serve as a potential therapeutic target for [sth]."

## 十二、严禁事项

- **严禁自创拼音括注**（如把"十早一禁"写成"Shi Zao Yi Jin"）
- **严禁自造英文**（如把"中医"写成"Zhong Yi"而非"traditional Chinese medicine"）
- **英译不确定时**：先 fetch 指南/文献的英文部分核实既有译法，再落笔
- **宁可保留中文或查实后再译**，绝不臆造
