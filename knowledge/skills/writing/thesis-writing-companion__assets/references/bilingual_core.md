## 3. 学术写作原则 / Academic Writing Principles

- **清晰高于华丽**：用强动词、短句、明确主语。Clarity beats ornament: use strong verbs, short sentences, explicit subjects.
- **展示论证，而非堆砌**：用 PEEL（Point 论点 → Evidence 证据 → Explanation 解释 → Link 回扣）。Show the argument, don't just stack citations.
- **谨慎表达（Hedging）**：相关≠因果；样本有限时标明局限。Be cautious: correlation ≠ causation; flag limitations.
- **先完成再完美**：初稿允许粗糙，重点是写出来。Draft ugly, then refine.
- **客观语气**：少用 "I think"，多用 "The evidence suggests"。Minimize "I think"; prefer "The evidence suggests".

## 5. 学术英语常用短语 / Common Academic Phrases (EN)

- 指出空白：*However, little research has been conducted on...*（然而，关于……的研究甚少）
- 陈述目的：*The primary aim of this study is to...*（本研究的主要目的是……）
- 呈现结果：*The results revealed that...*（结果显示……）
- 解释发现：*These findings suggest that...*（这些发现表明……）
- 指出局限：*Several limitations should be acknowledged.*（应承认若干局限）
- 未来研究：*Future research should examine...*（未来研究应考察……）

## 6. 学术诚信 / Academic Integrity

- **禁止**：代写（人/AI 生成核心内容）、数据造假、 mosaic 抄袭、自我抄袭（未标注重复发表）。
  Forbidden: ghostwriting (human/AI generating core content), data fabrication, mosaic plagiarism, self-plagiarism.
- **允许**：用 AI 辅助润色/翻译/语法检查、自己写初稿后让 AI 改写平淡表达（需深度编辑并核实引用）。
  Allowed: AI for polishing/translation/grammar; self-written draft refined with AI (deep edit + verify citations).
- **降重原则**：合法改写（同义替换、句式重组、图表化、规范引用），绝不用翻译机翻洗稿或插入乱码等作弊手段。
  Lower similarity legally: paraphrase, restructure, visualize, cite properly — never machine-spin or insert junk characters.

## 7. 通用自检清单 / Universal Checklist

- [ ] 研究问题能否一句话说清？ Can you state the RQ in one sentence?
- [ ] 文献是否涵盖奠基性与近 3–5 年前沿？ Foundational + recent (3–5 yr) literature covered?
- [ ] 方法是否与问题匹配并说明局限？ Method matches question; limitations stated?
- [ ] 引用是否完整、格式是否统一？ Citations complete and consistent?
- [ ] 是否报告效应量/置信区间（量化）？ Effect sizes / CIs reported (quant)?
- [ ] 查重与 AIGC 是否达标？ Similarity & AIGC within limits?
- [ ] 数据是否可获取/可复现？ Data available / reproducible?
